# Agent Guide — AMI'S Properties

## Logging In
Open `AMIS-Properties-agent-portal.html` and sign in with the email/password your admin
set up for you. Your account must already exist — agents don't self-register.

## Availability toggle
The switch in the top bar controls whether you're shown as available. This is purely a
status flag for now (no automated routing yet reacts to it).

## My Listings
Only your own properties — add, edit, publish/hide/mark sold or rented. You can't see or
touch another agent's listings; the backend enforces this, not just the UI.

Attach photos, a video tour, or a floor plan directly in the Add/Edit form.

## Leads & Bookings
Every inspection booking and price offer tied to your listings shows up here.
Confirm, cancel, or complete bookings as they progress.

## Messages
Real-time chat with customers who message you from one of your listings. The conversation
list shows the customer, the property, and the last message. Replies send instantly to
the customer's app.

## Reports
Export your own listings, leads, or a performance snapshot (with a commission estimate
based on your commission rate and your sold/rented listings) as CSV, Excel, or PDF.

## Things that are genuinely not live yet
- Typing indicators and read receipts in chat (the backend emits the event; nothing
  displays it yet).
- File/image sharing inside chat.
- A calendar view for bookings (currently a sorted list, not a calendar widget).
