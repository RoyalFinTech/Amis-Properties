# Customer Guide — AMI'S Properties

## Getting Started
1. Open the app, swipe through the intro screens (or tap Skip).
2. Enter your name and phone number — you'll get a 6-digit code by SMS.
3. Verify the code, then set a 4- or 6-digit PIN. That's your login from now on —
   next time, just enter your phone and PIN (no code needed unless it's a new device).

## Finding a property
- **Home** — featured listings and the latest published properties, filterable by category.
- **Search** — filter by keyword, purpose (sale/rent/lease/short stay), type, price range,
  bedrooms, bathrooms.
- Tap any listing for full details: photos, specs, amenities, nearby points of interest,
  a map, and the listing agent.

## On a property page
- **♡ Save** — adds it to your Saved tab.
- **📞 Call** / **💬 Message** / **WhatsApp icon** / **🔗 Share** — reach the agent directly,
  or message them inside the app (real-time chat, saved for later).
- **Mortgage calculator** — enter a down payment, rate, and term for an estimated monthly
  payment. This is a calculator only — it doesn't submit anything anywhere.
- **Schedule Inspection** — pick a date/time; the agent confirms from their side.

## Account tab
Your profile, your bookings, appearance/language settings, and a way to point the app at
a different server if you're testing against a different backend. Contact Us has your
direct line to AMI'S Properties (call, WhatsApp, email, directions).

## Notes
- Your PIN is never stored in plain text — the server only keeps a one-way hash of it.
- If you lose access, there's no self-service "forgot PIN" flow yet — contact support via
  WhatsApp or email (see Contact Us in the app).
